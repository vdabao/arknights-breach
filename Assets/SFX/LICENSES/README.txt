CRISP CARDS - Free Sampler
==========================

20 sound effects for card games, deckbuilders and casino tables.

GET THE FULL PACK
  This free sampler has 20 of the 105 sounds.
  The full pack adds the other 85: https://anipixels.itch.io/crisp-cards

FORMATS
  WAV/  44.1 kHz, 16-bit, stereo (for editing / highest quality)
  OGG/  Ogg Vorbis (small files, ready for Godot, Unity, GameMaker, web)

Every sound is loudness-matched, starts on the first sample (no dead air)
and peaks at or below -1 dBFS.

TIPS
  - Sounds ending in _01, _02, _03 are variations: pick one at random each time.
  - card_hover and ui_hover are quiet on purpose - they fire constantly.
  - Deal a hand with card_deal (or card_draw) every ~100 ms; the variations keep it from sounding robotic.
  - shuffle_riffle ends with the deck being squared on the table; shuffle_overhand is the quicker one.
  - turn_start, turn_end, victory, defeat, jackpot and lose are tuned stingers (C major / C minor).
